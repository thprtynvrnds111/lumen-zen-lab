# Zential Pure, Product Context

## Brand
Skin Intelligence brand. Not beauty, not skincare. Positioned alongside Whoop, Eight Sleep, AG1. Customers invest in inputs to living tissue. Instruments, not devices or tools.

## Register
brand

## Users

**The Invested Ritualist** (28–42, professional/creative)
- Owns 1–2 beauty devices. Reads ingredient labels. Cross-references claims. Wants mechanism before benefit. Clicks study links.
- Objection: brand credibility, not product skepticism.
- Language: "I want to understand how it actually works." / "What's the actual protocol?"

**The Clinic Dropout** (32–50)
- Had professional microcurrent/red light sessions, stopped for cost/inconvenience. Knows results are real. Wants home replication.
- Objection: does the device reach clinic-grade output?
- Low price sensitivity, was paying €80–120/session. "One clinic session, forever" converts her.

## Tone
Quiet authority. Calm, clinical, never hype-driven. Concentrated, not explained. Honest before flattering. Ritual gravity. No exclamations, no influencer-speak, no adverbs.

## Anti-References
NuFACE, FOREO, typical skincare DTC, avoid their visual language: millennial pink, before/after grids, transformation promises, exclamation copy, floating product-on-white hero shots.

## Strategic Principles
- Mechanism before benefit, benefit before claim
- No before/after imagery (positions as cosmetics, not Skin Intelligence)
- No fake urgency
- 14-Day Protocol Guarantee, on the purchase, not the result
- "Clinic-precision facial technology. €88 once." is the core value proposition

## Flagship Product
Face Introducer, red light (630–660nm), microcurrent, EMS, thermal. Four technologies. One instrument. €88.

## Visual Identity
- Fog canvas (#f5f5f7) backgrounds, white card surfaces
- Tangerine CTA color (#f69251 / hsl 24 90% 64%), sole action color
- Teal accent (#2ED8A8), secondary identity touches only
- Gold (#C6A07C), premium warmth
- Ink (#1d1d1f), Apple-level dark text
- Typography: DM Sans (sans) + Lora (serif), calm authority, editorial weight
- 24px card radius, 28px pill buttons, soft but not playful
- Tracking scale: tight/negative for headings (Apple-influenced)

## Content / Copy Rules
- No "Shop Now", use "Order the [Device Name]" or "See the science"
- No before/after imagery
- No "amazing", "game-changing", "glow up", "self-care", "transforms"
- Use: ritual, instrument, protocol, frequency, stimulates, supports, with consistent use
- Present tense for science claims
- Short sentences as punctuation, "€88. Once." "Per session."
